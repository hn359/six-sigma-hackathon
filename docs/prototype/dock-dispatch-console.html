Dock Dispatch Console

Five stations with 20 docks each and 100 bikes, 50 of them in the reserve depot at the start. Every station is checked every 15 minutes, which the replay runs as 5 seconds (change it with "15 min ="). A control-chart signal, or a station going outside 4–16 bikes, sends the van.

Simulated network and invented data, made to show the method in our SPEC. This page is not the tested tool.

1 · Night planHistory sets each station's 06:00 start so its normal day stays between 4 and 16 bikes.
2 · Check every 15 minI-chart of net flow minus that time slot's usual flow, with Western Electric rules 1-4.
3 · Signal or out of spec → vanThe van moves bikes from a station with spare or from the reserve, back to the expected path.
4 · Back in controlNext readings are in spec with no signal. The weekly p-chart tests whether the policy worked.
